About

I am a mathematician working in mathematical statistics. My research determines exact regions and sharp inequalities between dependence measures for copulas — in particular between Chatterjee's rank correlation ξ and classical measures of concordance such as Spearman's ρ, Kendall's τ, Spearman's footrule, Gini's γ and Blomqvist's β.

My articles are accompanied by Lean 4 formalizations of their main results, and I maintain open-source software for computing with copulas. I received my doctorate (Dr. rer. nat.) from the University of Freiburg in 2025 and studied mathematics at the University of Bonn. Alongside research, I work as a freelance data engineer (Python, data pipelines, LLM-based tooling).

Research interests

  • Copulas and dependence modeling
  • Exact (attainable) regions and sharp inequalities between rank correlations and dependence measures
  • Chatterjee's rank correlation and measures of functional dependence
  • Stochastic orderings, positive dependence, optimal transport methods for copula extremal problems
  • Formal verification of probabilistic and statistical results in Lean 4
